18.1.1 you have been tricked, manipulated or deceived into making the payment;

18.1.2 the payee is not the person you intended to pay or the payment is not for the purpose you intended; and

18.1.3 the payment was made on or after 7 October 2024.

18.2 You will not be eligible for reimbursement where:

18.2.1 the payment was made using your Card;

18.2.2 the payment was made to another account you or your Authorised User controls;

18.2.3 the payment was made for illegal purposes;

18.2.4 the payment was made to an account with a national savings bank, credit union or municipal bank in the UK; or

18.2.5 the payment related to a civil dispute (e.g., where you paid a legitimate supplier but were dissatisfied with the quality of the goods or services).

18.3 You should report any suspected APP Scams to us and the police as soon as possible (and at least within thirteen (13) months of the payment). 18.4 We will only reimburse eligible transactions from your claim. When your claim is closed, we will inform you of the transactions we can reimburse and any that are ineligible. The maximum amount we can reimburse you for an APP Scam claim is up to the value and excess as set by Applicable Laws.
